Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for day-to-day operation and sensor calibration.

A sensor shows no reading. What should I check first?
Confirm the device is publishing to the Aurae sensor topic and that its client ID is unique. The dashboard only renders a value once a message arrives.
How often do the boxes on the dashboard update?
Telemetry values refresh dynamically every 5 seconds or whenever a broker telemetry payload syncs with the database.
Where do exported records go?
Exported CSV files and graphs are saved directly to your local browser download directory.
