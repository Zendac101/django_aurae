How to read this telemetry data

  • PM2.5 above 25 µg/m³ for more than 20 minutes triggers an immediate zone alert.
  • NO₂ is measured in ppb and typically peaks during evening commuter traffic hours.
  • CO stays low indoors; a drifting baseline usually indicates a blocked or obstructed sensor intake.
